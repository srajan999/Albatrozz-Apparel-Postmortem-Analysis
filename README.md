Albatrozz Apparel — Business Post-Mortem Analysis
By Srajan Choudhary — Founder & Business Operations Lead (2022–2024)

PURPOSE
This workbook analyses two years of Albatrozz Apparel's own operating data to identify what drove sales
growth in Year 1 and the subsequent plateau/decline in Year 2.

DATA SOURCE & METHODOLOGY
Formal Shopify/Meta Ads exports were not retained after the venture closed. Monthly figures are
RECONSTRUCTED from the founder's own operating recollection, calibrated to remembered totals
(~2 years active, ~900-1000 orders, ~Rs.500-1000 AOV, ~Rs.5,000-15,000/month ad spend, an expanding
catalog). Treat absolute figures as directionally realistic; the PATTERNS are the analytical takeaway.

HOW THIS WORKBOOK IS BUILT
Every chart and KPI on the Dashboard tab is formula-driven off the Data tab — change any blue input
cell in Data and every card and chart recalculates automatically. Grey-shaded rows on the Data tab are
helper calculations that feed the charts; leave their formulas intact.

TABS
Data       - monthly inputs (blue) + calculated metrics (black, formula-driven) + chart helper tables
Dashboard  - 6 live KPI cards + 9 charts covering growth, efficiency, retention, and seasonality
Insights   - root-cause analysis and recommendations
